# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-03_

This is the privacy policy for **Timeout**, a party game app developed by Tom Hohler ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

## What we collect

**Nothing personal.** The app has no user accounts, no sign-up, no profiles. We do not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information.

**Anonymous usage statistics.** The app sends a few game events (the app opened or closed, a game started, a round or a game finished, a game abandoned, a deck opened, notifications accepted or refused, a notification opened) with the deck played, the number of players and teams, and the game's length, plus the app version, OS and language. They go to PostHog, hosted in the EU. They carry no identifier: nothing is stored on your device to recognize it, there is no user profile, your IP address is not used to locate you, and two games played on the same phone can't be linked together. Player names and card contents are never sent. These statistics are kept for at most 25 months, then deleted.

## What we don't collect

- No tracking — no account, no device or advertising identifier, nothing that could tie the statistics above back to you.
- No advertising identifiers, no third-party trackers, no marketing partners.
- No microphone access, no camera access, no contacts access, no location access.

## What's stored on your device

- Game state (current round, scores, players you've added) is stored locally and never leaves your device.
- The deck catalog (lists of celebrities, characters, etc.) is fetched from our server and cached locally.

## What's stored on our server

- The deck catalog. This is shared content used by all players — it does not include any of your data.
- The anonymous usage statistics described above, on PostHog's EU servers.

## Permissions we ask for

- **Notifications (optional):** offered after you finish a game, used only to announce new seasonal decks. The notifications are scheduled on your device from the deck catalog: no push token or identifier is created, and nothing is sent anywhere.
- **Motion (iOS) / accelerometer (Android):** used during player setup to scatter players across the screen when you shake the phone. Motion data is processed only on your device and is never transmitted.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated date at the top will reflect the change. Significant changes will be announced in the app's release notes.
