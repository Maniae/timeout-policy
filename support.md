# Support

**Timeout** is a personal project — there's no helpdesk, but if you have a question, a bug report, or a deck idea, send an email and you'll get a reply.

- **Email:** tom.hohler.dev@gmail.com

## Reporting a bug

Please include:
- Device model and OS version
- App version (Settings → About in the app, or check the app store listing)
- What you were doing when the bug appeared
- What you expected vs. what happened
